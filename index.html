About Appu Gaming

Description

Appu Gaming shares mods for Euro Truck Simulator 2: bus mods, skins, traffic, templates, truck mods, maps and plugins. Every download link opens on modsbase.com.

Contact

Write to Appu Gaming at . Please include the mod name and its category.

Help
  1. Pick a category on the left.
  2. Tap Download on a mod. It opens modsbase.com.
  3. Download the file there and copy it to Documents/Euro Truck Simulator 2/mod.
  4. Enable it in the game’s Mod Manager.
Customer support

A link is broken, or a mod will not load? Email us the category, the mod name and what went wrong, and Appu Gaming will fix it.

Privacy policy

Appu Gaming does not ask for your name, phone number or account. The mod names and links you add, and your settings, stay in your own browser. Download links open modsbase.com, which has its own privacy policy.
